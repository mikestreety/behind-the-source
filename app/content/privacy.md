**Behind the Source** is run by [Mike Street](https://www.mikestreety.co.uk/). This page explains what personal data the site collects and what happens to it.

## Analytics

Page views are counted with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/), a privacy-friendly analytics service. It does not set cookies or track you across other sites, and it does not collect personal information. It records things like the page visited, the referring site, browser, screen size and an approximate country.

The site is served through [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/), which processes your IP address and request details in order to deliver the pages and protect the site. Cloudflare Web Analytics is also used to measure page performance; it does not use cookies. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Newsletter

If you [sign up to the newsletter](/signup/), your email address is stored by [Mailchimp](https://mailchimp.com/) and used only to send you Behind the Source updates. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask for your details to be removed at any time. See [Mailchimp's privacy policy](https://www.intuit.com/privacy/statement/).

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete any personal data held about you. Email <!--email_off-->[behindthesource@mikestreety.co.uk](mailto:behindthesource@mikestreety.co.uk)<!--/email_off--> and I'll sort it. If you're unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).
